About

A local-story AR company

Epispace revives the myths, legends, and folktales tucked inside a place, and sets them back on that ground in AR, so people walk the lanes and step into the shops. We opened on 25 May 2025. The registered office is 302-D, 3F, 86 Jungjeong-ro, Seogwi-dong, Seogwipo, Jeju Special Self-Governing Province. We are a tenant at Seogwipo Startup Bay.

Visitors are not there for the Web-AR itself. They stop for the story, they walk because of the story, and a shopping street wants that walk to become a sale. NFC and Web-AR are how the experience opens in a browser, with no app to install.

In April 2025, the Korea Tourism Organization selected Epispace as a Preliminary Tourism Venture (예비관광벤처기업).

Business

A story that becomes a sale on the street

We place stories with local governments, shopping streets, destinations, and festival planners.

Local story AR trails

Neighborhood commerce

We turn a district's lesser-known myths, legends, and folktales into stops along the street. A tap on an NFC tag opens an AR mission in the browser. Finish the mission, and a partner-shop coupon follows.

We have run this on Lee Jung-seop Street in Seogwipo, the cultural lane named for the painter Lee Jung-seop. The same pattern can cover nearly all of Jeju. Clients: local governments, shopping streets, and destinations.

Virtual out-of-home

Virtual OOH

Brand and story advertising placed on the street in AR, without a physical sign or installation. Clients: shopping districts, local brands, and public destination campaigns.

Festival and event AR

Photo spots and missions

Photo spots and missions for festivals, pop-ups, and offline events. The cherry blossom tunnel is one example.

Technology

Three pieces that open the story on site

What a visitor meets is a folktale, a mission, and a coupon. These platforms are how that sequence runs without an app.

NFC ONE-TAP

No app. Tap the NFC tag on the street and the browser opens into Web-AR.

WEB AR storytelling

Jeju myths, endangered wildflowers, and historical figures appear on the real street. The story of that place continues in front of you.
