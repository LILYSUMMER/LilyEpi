About

We put the story back on the street

Lee Jung-seop Street had the painter's name, and not much reason to stay. Call it a themed street if you like. Without a story to enjoy, people just walk through. We opened in Seogwipo to set that place's story back into the gap.

People do not stop for the technology. They stop for the story of the lane. There is no app. Tap the NFC tag outside a shop and the browser opens. When the story ends, they walk into the shop next door.

Technology

How a walk goes

  1. 01

    Tap the NFC tag

    Hold a phone to the tag outside a shop and the story opens right in the browser.

  2. 02

    Follow the story

    A character appears on the street and asks you to help find a missing bull. You follow the arrow and the distance to the next stop.

  3. 03

    Step into a shop

    A letter opens when you arrive, and a finished mission leads to a partner-shop coupon.
