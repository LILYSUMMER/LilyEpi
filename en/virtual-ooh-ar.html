An ad over the street, with no sign to put up

We put a sign for a sample shop, Gyulbit Café, over Robson Street in Vancouver. Hold up your phone there and you’ll see it floating in place. Anywhere else, it appears in front of you.

  1. Scan the QR code below with your phone camera.
  2. Tap Start and allow camera and location access.
  3. Follow the arrow on the screen to find the sign.
