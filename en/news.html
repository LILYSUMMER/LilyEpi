21 October 2026

YUNSEUL got a name

Today, she got a name. The being in the puddle could be called, for the first time. YUNSEUL. It is the word for the light that flickers on water.

YUNSEUL is a being of light that crossed from the sea of Jeju to the puddles of Vancouver. It murmurs in light instead of words, and only AR can translate what it says. This is EPISPACE’s own character IP.

New episodes every Thursday on @epispace.official. The world is also taking shape as an AI short. The name is here. The story starts now.

Watch on Instagram

15 October 2026

LOOK DOWN: point the camera at the ground on a rainy day

On a rainy day, point the camera at the ground and the puddle gathers again. YUNSEUL looks up from the surface and says, in one line, what you will lose tonight.

LOOK DOWN is the WebAR that sets YUNSEUL on a real Vancouver floor. Nothing to install. The browser is the screen. Brighter light fades her; darker light makes her clear. Take a photo and she stays in the puddle with you.

A company that started in Seogwipo now opens the same IP over Vancouver rain. The place can change. The story should stay on the ground.

Open LOOK DOWN

15 October 2026

An ad over the street, with no sign to put up

We made an ad that floats over the street, with no sign to put up. Two places: Robson Street in Vancouver, and Lee Jung-seop Street in Seogwipo, where we first opened. Over both of them we hung a sample shop, Gyulbit Café.

Stand on either street and you will see it in place. Open it anywhere else and it appears in front of you. Nothing to install. The phone browser is the screen.

Tap the sign and the shop opens

Tap the sign and the shop card opens. You can switch the language to Korean, English, Chinese, or Japanese. We also let a cup of tangerine tea float there instead.

It hides behind buildings and trees

A sign that punches through a facade is hard to believe is on the street. On Robson Street we use that street’s buildings and street trees, and hide the sign wherever something other than sky sits in front of it. The alley on Lee Jung-seop Street reads buildings the same way.

Gyulbit Café and its offer are made up for this sample. The place, the words, and the language can change at any time.

21 November 2025

We stood at 2025 The Next Step

On 21 November we took part in 2025 The Next Step. Near the end of the year, a company made in Seogwipo carried its street stories back into a room in the same city.

This is not an award announcement. It is the day we stood in front of people and said again what we had been doing in the lanes.

November 2025

We opened offline AR on Lee Jung-seop Street

From June through December 2025 we walked people through Lee Jung-seop Street in Seogwipo with Web AR. In November we opened that experience as offline AR, for free.

Tap the NFC tag outside a shop and the browser opens. Nothing to install. A story sits on the real street inside the camera. Finish the mission and a partner shop’s coupon appears.

After half a year of the pilot, November was the month a story that never made the map stood on the lane.

Open the Lee Jung-seop Street Web AR

25 October 2025

A tent by the sea, at the youth camp

On 25 October we opened a booth at the Seogwipo Youth Ieum Camp. We put up a white tent on the grass facing the sea, and wrote on the valance the sentence we wanted this lane to hear.

Lee Jung-seop’s Fantasy of Seogwipo, in your hands. EPISPACE.

24 September 2025

We stood with Samwol Port

On 24 September, Samwol Port and EPISPACE signed a working agreement. We work with local governments, shopping streets, destinations, and local brands. Samwol Port is a partner who sat down at the table with us.

23–24 September 2025

At Ieum Week, we put our street on stage

On 23 and 24 September, EPISPACE stood at the Korea Tourism Organization’s Ieum Week demo day. Sooryeon Yoon told our street from the stage. At the booth beside it we set down the same panels we use in the lane.

2 September 2025

We filed for the way an NFC tag opens a story

On 2 September we filed a patent application: a method of delivering AR content from an NFC tag. It is not yet granted. We submitted the method. So we do not say we received a patent. We say we filed.

Tap the tag on the street, the browser opens, the story sits on the camera. The filing is about that first gesture.

15 July 2025

Halla Ilbo wrote down how we started

On 15 July, Halla Ilbo wrote the beginning of EPISPACE. “Stories and technology, bringing life to Lee Jung-seop Street in Seogwipo.”

“People said Jeju was a good place to start, and that there was room. While looking for the idea, I walked Lee Jung-seop Street. A themed street needs something to do, and this one looked short on that. That was the start.”

Sooryeon Yoon, in Halla Ilbo

We did not go to Lee Jung-seop Street to sell a technology. We saw a lane without a story, and opened a company to bring the story back.

Read it in Halla Ilbo

6–7 June 2025

Our first booth, at the summer flea market

On 6 and 7 June we opened EPISPACE’s first booth at the Jeju flea market. The company was not yet two weeks old. When people came over we did not talk about apps. We said, tap the tag and a story opens.

April 2025

We became a preliminary tourism venture

In April 2025 the Korea Tourism Organization selected EPISPACE for the 16th Preliminary Tourism Venture cohort. The category is immersive tourism content. The work is named as a service that wakes themed streets with NFC and augmented reality.

A selection is not a prize. It is a name for the work. We opened the door on 25 May. Sooryeon Yoon is the representative. The room is 302-D, third floor, 86 Jungjeong-ro, Seogwipo.
