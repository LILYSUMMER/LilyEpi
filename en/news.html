21 November 2025

Joined 2025 The Next Step

On 21 November 2025, Epispace, a local-story AR company in Seogwipo, Jeju, took part in 2025 The Next Step. The date is written in the final review deck for the 16th Preliminary Tourism Venture cohort, run by the Korea Tourism Organization.

What the photos show

The left side is a wall of participating organizations and companies, and the Epispace logo is on it. The right side is the booth printed with that date in the same deck: printed matter and small models on a table. The deck calls the entry “participation in 2025 The Next Step.” It does not call it an award or a signed contract.

Why this date is on the record

After selection in April 2025, Epispace’s named public dates run through a flea-market booth, a demo day, a youth camp, a partnership signing, and this November event. If someone asks what the company did in November, the answer on the record is the offline AR launch on Lee Jung-seop Street and 2025 The Next Step.

What Epispace is

Epispace puts a place’s myths and folktales back on that ground in Web-AR, so people walk the lane and step into the shops. There is no app. An NFC tag opens the browser. The registered office is 302-D, 3F, 86 Jungjeong-ro, Seogwi-dong, Seogwipo, and the company is a tenant at Seogwipo Startup Bay.

November 2025

Offline AR launched on Lee Jung-seop Street

From June through December 2025, Epispace ran a Web AR tourism pilot on Lee Jung-seop Street in Seogwipo, the cultural lane named for the painter Lee Jung-seop. In November 2025 the company launched offline AR there and sold paid sessions.

Pilot and launch

The tourism-commercialization table in the final review lists “Lee Jung-seop Street Web AR tourism-content pilot” for June–December 2025, marked in progress at the time the deck was written. The paid offline launch is November 2025. The pilot is the half-year of on-site operation. November is when that work opened as a paid session.

What the screens are

The photograph above is the “service screens” slide in the same deck. The slide’s own caption says the browser AR includes an intro, route guidance, interactive pieces, sharing, a current-location marker, and a shop-linked discount coupon. One screen asks the visitor to walk Lee Jung-seop Street further. Discount figures printed inside those screens are examples on the screen, not revenue.

How a visitor enters

Visitors do not install an app. They tap an NFC tag, the browser opens, and Web-AR starts. The offer to a shopping street is that a finished mission can continue into a partner-shop coupon. Clients are local governments, shopping streets, and destinations.

25 October 2025

AR booth at the Seogwipo Youth Ieum Camp

On 25 October 2025, Epispace ran an AR tourism booth at the Seogwipo Youth Ieum Camp, “Connecting youth and the local.” The 16th Preliminary Tourism Venture final review marks that date complete.

The sentence on the tent

The valance reads, in Korean, that Lee Jung-seop’s “Fantasy of Seogwipo” is in your hands, and then the name Epispace. Inside are a monitor, a table, and chairs, with the sea behind the tent. The review records the event name, the date, and “AR tourism booth.” It does not give a street address.

How the booth fits the work

Epispace’s work is not a talk about technology. It puts the lane’s story back on the lane. The camp booth hung that sentence on a tent and opened an AR session in the field. The following month, November, paid offline AR opened on Lee Jung-seop Street.

Who operated it

Epispace operated the booth. The review names the Seogwipo Youth Ieum Camp as the event. The representative is Sooryeon Yoon, and the company is a tenant at Seogwipo Startup Bay.

23–24 September 2025

Korea Tourism Organization Ieum Week demo day

From 23 to 24 September 2025, Epispace exhibited AR tourism content at the Korea Tourism Organization’s Ieum Week demo day. The 16th-cohort final review marks the dates complete and names the Korea Tourism Organization in the notes.

The name on the screen

The stage screen reads “2025 TOURISM CONNECT WEEK” and “Epispace, representative Sooryeon Yoon.” The right-hand photo is the exhibit printed with that entry: panels and a small installation. The review’s event name is the Ieum Week demo day. The English line on the screen is Tourism Connect Week. This page treats those as two labels for the same dates, not as two events.

What was taken to the demo day

What Epispace took was Web-AR without an app. An NFC tag opens the browser, and the story of that street is laid on the camera. The selection category is immersive tourism content. The project name is an NFC and AR service that activates themed neighborhood streets.

Where the demo day sits in the year

The demo day comes after the April selection and before the October youth camp and the November offline launch. Between the early flea-market booth and the street launch, it is the record of showing the content on a Korea Tourism Organization program.

2 September 2025

Patent application for NFC-based AR

On 2 September 2025, Epispace filed a patent application titled, in translation, “a method of providing AR content based on an NFC tag.” This is a filing, not a granted patent. The quantitative page of the 16th Preliminary Tourism Venture final review lists one patent application, dated 2 September 2025.

What a filing means

A filing means the method was submitted. It does not mean examination has finished or that a right has been confirmed. The site therefore does not say “we received a patent.” It says “we filed.”

Where the method touches the work

An Epispace session starts when someone taps an NFC tag. The tag opens the browser, a Web-AR mission follows, and a partner-shop coupon can follow the mission. The title of the filing points at that first step: delivering AR content from an NFC tag.

Other figures in the same review

The achieved results in that review also include 500 customers, 1 new hire, and 1 beta release. Revenue is omitted from this website at the company’s request. This page also does not compare those figures with the original targets.

15 July 2025

Halla Ilbo on starting a company from Jeju stories

On 15 July 2025, Halla Ilbo profiled Sooryeon Yoon, founder of Epispace, on starting the company in Jeju. The line kept here from the coverage is: “Stories and technology, bringing life to Lee Jung-seop Street in Seogwipo.”

What the article confirms

The article notes selection as a Korea Tourism Organization preliminary tourism venture in April 2025, tenancy at Seogwipo Startup Bay, and the use of NFC and augmented reality on Lee Jung-seop Street. Those three points line up with the business registration and with the 16th-cohort final review. The company opened on 25 May 2025. The office is 302-D, 3F, 86 Jungjeong-ro, Seogwi-dong, Seogwipo.

What the founder said in the article

“People said Jeju was a good place to start, and that there was room. While looking for the idea, I walked Lee Jung-seop Street. A themed street needs something to do, and this one looked short on that. That was the start.”

Sooryeon Yoon, Epispace, Halla Ilbo, 15 July 2025

How the quotation sits with the company

Epispace’s view is that visitors are not there for Web-AR as a technology. They stop for the story, they walk because of the story, and a shopping street wants that walk to become a visit to a shop. The Halla Ilbo quotation is the founder saying that judgment started on Lee Jung-seop Street.

6–7 June 2025

XR booth at the Jeju flea market

From 6 to 7 June 2025, Epispace ran an XR booth at the Jeju Special Self-Governing Province flea market. The 16th-cohort final review lists it as “XR experience booth linked to the Jeju provincial flea market” and marks it complete.

What the photograph shows

EPISPACE hangs over the booth. The banner behind the table reads “2025 제주 국가유산 방문의 해,” the Jeju heritage visiting year. On the table are printed sheets, small tools, and a laptop. The review does not give a headcount or revenue for this booth, so this page does not either.

The first public booth after selection

Epispace was selected in April 2025 and opened on 25 May 2025. This booth, in early June, is the record of an XR session at a provincial flea market immediately after that. The Ieum Week demo day, the youth camp, and the November street launch follow in the same year.

What the booth was demonstrating

The company’s basic method is not an installed app. An NFC tag opens the browser, and Web-AR lays the story of that place on the camera. The flea-market booth was a date when that method was shown across a table.

The office is 302-D, 3F, 86 Jungjeong-ro, Seogwi-dong, and the company is a tenant at Seogwipo Startup Bay. What the selection was aimed at

Epispace sets local myths, legends, and folktales back on their ground in AR. The aim is not a technology demonstration. It is that a person walks the lane and enters a shop. NFC ONE-TAP opens the browser, WEB AR storytelling lays the story on the street, and a partner-shop coupon can follow a mission.
